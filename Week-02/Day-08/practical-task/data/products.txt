Sample Product Information
Document Type: Products
Version: 1.0

Product A is a customer management web application.

The application allows users to create customer records, update customer information, search customer records, and view customer details.

Product B is an AI knowledge assistant.

The AI knowledge assistant allows users to ask questions about approved documents. The system retrieves relevant document sections and provides them as context to a language model.

The knowledge assistant should provide source information when answering questions.

The system should avoid creating information that is not supported by the available documents.
